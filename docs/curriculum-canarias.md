# Canarian Primary curriculum and course size

Why Chinijo's course page is designed the way it is, based on the Canarian
education regulations. Facts from the regulations come first, with their source;
estimates drawn from them are marked **Inference**. Sources were consulted on
2026-10-08 through ATE's index of Canarian education regulations
(https://ateeducacion.github.io/normativa_educativa_canaria/llms.txt).

"Verified" means the passage was read in the official text (BOC PDF or BOC page).
"Corpus" means it was taken from the ATE corpus summary of the official text and
has not been re-read in the official text for this document.

## What the regulations say

### Areas in 1.º and 2.º (D211 art. 8; verified)

- Every course, every learner (art. 8.5): Conocimiento del Medio Natural, Social y
  Cultural; Educación Artística; Educación Física; Lengua Castellana y Literatura;
  Lengua Extranjera; Matemáticas.
- Every course, as a transversal subject (art. 8.7): Educación Emocional y para la
  Creatividad.
- Religión or Atención educativa: 60 minutes a week in every year (art. 9.4).
- Only in the third cycle (5.º and 6.º): Educación en Valores Cívicos y Éticos
  (art. 8.6) and Segunda Lengua Extranjera (art. 8.8).

A learner in 1.º or 2.º therefore has **8 areas**.

### Weekly timetable (D211 art. 9 and Anexo 4, page 45859 of the BOC; verified)

At least 25 hours a week including a 30-minute daily break, and at least 175
school days a year; sessions of 55 minutes (preferred) or 45 minutes.

### How teaching is organised

- The teaching team plans **situaciones de aprendizaje** (learning situations) to
  develop the competences; Anexo 3, which frames their design, is not prescriptive
  (D211 art. 6.6; verified).
- Primary has three two-year cycles; 1.º and 2.º are the first cycle (D211 art. 8;
  corpus). Every evaluation criterion of the cycle is worked and assessed in each
  of the two years (INS26 Primera; corpus).
- Evaluation sessions at least once a trimester, with a report each trimester
  (OEV arts. 5 and 11; corpus). Grades in Primary are qualitative (IN, SU, BI, NT,
  SB) and **numeric grades are not used** (OEV art. 11.3, INS26 Segunda.6; corpus).
- **No regulation sets a number of learning situations, units or activities per
  area, trimester or year.**

### Diversity and NEAE (corpus)

- Types of NEAE include specific educational needs (disability, severe conduct
  disorder, autism), specific learning difficulties, ADHD, late incorporation,
  communication and language difficulties, and high abilities (D25 art. 11).
- Support is given in the ordinary classroom by preference (D25 art. 4.3).
  Curricular adaptations start from the group's own learning situations
  (D25 art. 18; INS26 Primera.11), so learners with NEAE normally use the same
  courses as their group.

### Digital environments and accessibility (corpus)

- Virtual learning environments of publicly funded schools must promote universal
  accessibility and design for all (LOE art. 111 bis.2).
- Usability and accessibility of technologies (D25 art. 17.4); teaching materials
  and assessment tools follow Universal Design for Learning (D211 art. 18.3, INS26).
- Digital competence in the first cycle refers to guided, supervised use of
  devices and virtual platforms (D211 Anexo 1 and Anexo 2).

## Inference: expected course size

- **Courses per learner.** With one Moodle course per area, 8 courses (9 if Música
  and Plástica are separate); in practice probably 5 to 8 in use. With one course
  per class group, 1 course plus a few for specialist teachers.
- **Sections per course.** If each section is a learning situation, about 3 to 12
  sections per area course over the year (more in Lengua, Matemáticas and Medio,
  fewer in Emocional and Religión); a class-group course could reach 8 to 20.
- **Activities per section.** About 3 to 8 activities per learning situation at
  this age, much of the work being offline and guided; the theme must still cope
  with about 15.
- **Activities per course.** Roughly 20 to 100 activities with completion over
  the year.

## What Chinijo does with this

| Fact or inference | Design decision |
|---|---|
| A course holds several learning situations, each a section, and 20–100 activities a year. | "My path" draws the **current section** (the section of the next activity), with its name, its pictogram, a link to it and "2 of 4 done here", plus the whole-course count. A path of every activity of the year would be too long for a 6-year-old. |
| A learning situation usually has 3–8 activities, sometimes up to about 15. | Up to 7 activities are drawn at once, from two before the next one; "N more" shows what is left out. The path scrolls in its own box on small screens. |
| Guided, supervised use of platforms in the first cycle. | One clear next step: "Next" with a single large Start link, "Back to the course" on every activity page, and encouragement with a "Keep going" link. |
| Primary grades are qualitative; no numeric grades. | Progress is shown as activities done ("3 of 8 activities done"), never as a percentage or a mark. |
| Learners with NEAE use the same courses, with adaptations. | Help is personal and chosen by the learner (display settings, ready-made combinations, "Listen"); teachers cannot impose it, and nothing records needs or diagnoses. |
| Accessibility and design for all are required of virtual learning environments. | WCAG 2.2 AA target, automated checks in CI and a manual audit plan (`docs/accessibility.md`). |

Not changed by the theme: the dashboard and "My courses" (5 to 9 course cards per
learner) are Moodle's own; course images set by teachers help learners recognise
each area there.
